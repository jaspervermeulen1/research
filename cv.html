Profile

My doctoral research examines how people shape human-robot collaboration, focusing on multi-actor work in robot-assisted orthopaedic surgery and the gap between system design and everyday practice.

The thesis, Understanding the Human(s) in Human-Robot Collaboration: Multi-Actor Work in Robot-Assisted Surgery, draws on video analysis of robot-assisted surgeries, 100+ hours of ethnographic observation in a Brisbane hospital, and interviews with surgical team members and Mako Product Specialists in Australia and New Zealand. I am in the final stages of preparing my thesis for examination.
